# For testers (PT-071, R24). The text of the "For testers" panel on the title screen and what a tester is sent with the link. Nothing here is a promise about the finished game.

## What this is
HUSHFALL is a first-person shooter that runs in your browser: 68 maps in two campaigns. It is a release candidate, not a finished game. Nobody outside the maker has played it end to end, so the numbers (damage, ammunition, how hard a fight is) are first guesses and you are the first real test of them.

## What to do
Play on the difficulty you are comfortable with (Easy is a fair place to start). Play a few maps, as many as you like, in any order your save allows. You do not need to finish. Stop whenever you stop having a reason to continue: **why you stopped is the most useful thing you can tell us.**

## The five questions
After each map, if the play log is on, the screen asks five things (all optional; a note is optional too):
1. Did I always know where to go?
2. Did I win a fight by moving, not just by shooting?
3. Did anything surprise me?
4. Was there a place I wanted to come back to?
5. Did it drag?

## The play log (off by default)
Settings has **Keep a play log on this device**. When it is on, the game writes down, on your device only, which maps you played, how each ended and how long it took, how much damage you took, how smoothly it ran (frame times and the render width), your browser's graphics card name, and your answers to the five questions. It contains no name, no email and nothing that identifies you. **Nothing is sent anywhere.** When you are done, press **Export play log**: you get a small text file. Read it if you like; send it to the person who gave you this link. **Clear play log** deletes it.

## What is known and not done (do not report these)
- The second half of the computer opponent, a credits roll, painted cutscenes (the story scenes are short camera moves), full colour-blind palettes.
- The numbers are untested by anyone else. If a fight feels unfair, that is a finding: say which map and where.
- Performance on weak machines is not measured: if it stutters, say what you run it on (the log records the graphics card). The game lowers its own render width if frames run slow; you can turn that off in Settings.

## What to report beyond the log
A crash or a black screen (the game shows a "Something broke" screen with a **Copy** button: paste what it copies); a map you could not finish; a line of text you could not read or did not understand; anything that made you stop.
