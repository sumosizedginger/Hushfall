# Store copy, first draft (PT-071, R23). Not published anywhere. Numbers come from `design/store/FACTS.md` (counted by `tools/dev/store-facts.mjs`); TO CONFIRM marks what only the owner can decide.

## Name and line
**HUSHFALL**
An old-school shooter. Every picture painted by code, every sound synthesised.

## Short description (one paragraph, for a capsule or a search result)
Port Marrow stops running when the Hush begins. You are the one person in town who cannot hear it. Fight through 68 maps in two campaigns, from the harbour quay to the bottom of a dying world, with 15 weapons, three kinds of ammunition and six weapon mods, against creatures that used to be your neighbours.

## Long description
Port Marrow, the first night of the Hush. A low tone has gone out over the town and everyone who hears it has stopped where they stood. You are Ines Calder, a Tide-Warden, and you cannot hear it: your father damaged one of your ears when you were a child. You hear what it does.

HUSHFALL is a first-person shooter in the old tradition: levels you learn, keys and switches, secrets behind walls that ring hollow, a handful of guns you swap between and learn to use well, and enemies that make you move. It is built map by map, not generated.

- **68 maps in two campaigns.** Campaign One takes you from the harbour to the Gate in four episodes; Campaign Two takes you through it and down. Six secret maps, seven bosses.
- **15 weapons**: nine guns and six melee weapons, found on the maps and not handed out. Three kinds of ammunition (fire, cold, piercing) change how a gun plays, and six mods change one for good.
- **Creatures with jobs.** 21 kinds, each with a tell you can read: the one that sings in bursts, the one in plate that charges, the one that raises the dead, the one that mends the others, the one that takes aim and waits.
- **Levels with a mechanic each** and a few that combine them: breathing doors, launch plates, airlocks, mirrors that carry a beam, a road that falls away behind you.
- **A painted look, made by code.** Every picture is baked from p5.js recipes; every sound is synthesised in the browser. Nothing is sampled, stock or taken from another game.
- **Plays in the browser** with a keyboard and mouse or a controller. Three difficulties, each explained in numbers. A text-size setting, sound captions, a reduce-flashing setting, a remappable everything.

## What the page must not say
That the game is "fast-paced", "intense", "immersive" or "unforgettable"; that it has a feature it does not have (no multiplayer, no level editor, no voice acting, no mod support); a play time that nobody has measured (the bot walks a map in one to three minutes, a person takes several times as long, and nobody outside the builder has played it end to end).
