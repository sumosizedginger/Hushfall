# Episode 2 — The Salt Works (design brief)

Intent, not status: status is derived from `validation/maps/<ID>.json`.

Theme (from `CAMPAIGN_MANIFEST.json`): industrial evaporation works and rail yards repurposed as processing. Look: salt-white and bone, rust, soot, oxidised-copper teal; open flats under a pale sky, brine channels, corrugated sheds, salt-caked brick. The Episode 2 theme kit (three skins so far, `tools/baker/recipes_e2.js`, seeds in `ART_BIBLE.md`): `L` salt-caked brick, `K` rusted corrugated sheet, `l` the salt crust of the pans.

## Maps

**M01 Brine Gate (main): Checkpoint assault across open ground.** The processing site is guarded and organised. Scale class MIXED (44% roofed, sightlines under 18 m, walls 4.2 m); introduces the FORTIFIED approach (a gate that only a switch in a side building opens) and the first machinery of the Works on screen from the first room. Version 3 (revised after the owner's PT-008 feedback; versions 1-2 were one 44 x 38 m open pan with 6 m walls and a roofless yard). The route: the ARRIVAL SHED (two cradles on a rail: something is wrong in the first room) -> a door onto a short open PAN with a plank bridge over one brine channel -> the north ditch (slow, covered, unseen by the towers) -> the GUARDHOUSE (the kennels open as you enter; the sluice wheel is on its south wall) -> out across the pan, cover to cover, past the salt dyke (a line of crates that cuts the line of sight, so the rank, the towers and the gate are met one engagement at a time) -> the GATE (rolled up by the wheel; pulling it wakes the near half of the hall) -> the receiving HALL (roofed, columns, beams, a cradle rail and pods overhead; the Warden-Graft and its Gaunt wait at the far end with a pillar on its charge lane) -> the GRAFTING BAY (resin walls, pods) -> the exit. Optional: the PUMP HOUSE on the south side (a cache, two sleepers) with a SECRET valve room behind its east wall. Only existing enemies, weapons and kit. 30 enemies (18 Tollbearers, 9 Gaunts, 2 Bellhands, the Warden-Graft); a perfect bot needs about 83 s on normal (measured by verify-map, three seeds); the owner decides the par. Look: the Salt Works.

(Indoor and outdoor are not the same thing in this engine: a floor skin of kind `floor` (planks, tile, grate, carpet, flag, silt `n`) gets a CEILING, a kind `outdoor` skin (cobble, pier planks, slate, silt `m`, salt crust) does not, and a `lamp` prop hangs from the ceiling height. A bridge of the indoor grate floated a ceiling slab in the sky and the road lamps floated at 6 m (PT-007); `tools/maplint.mjs` now fails both. Use `p` for a plank bridge, `j` lampposts and `n` lanterns in the open, and a `put` over an occupied cell replaces what was there without a word.)

(Design lessons from building it, kept for the next maps: an open field of idle enemies that all see each other is a meat grinder for any player who aims worse than the bot, so break sightlines (the dyke); put the ammunition ON the route the first approach takes; keep the entrance to a safe place out of the sight and range of anything that shoots; the robustness gate (a strafing bot with aim error) is the one that finds an unfair map, the perfect bot never does.)

**M02 Evaporation Pans (main): Open salt flats with hazard channels.** Hunting parties working the pans. Scale class SET-PIECE (10% roofed; the episode's one open crossing, never the opener); its reason is a CROSSING UNDER PURSUIT: the open ground has a job. Introduces the HUNTED CROSSING: a hunting party released behind you across open ground, and toxic brine channels that slow and burn (crossed on planks, or waded at a cost). Also introduces the HARPOON RIFLE (weapon 4; owner's go 2026-10-06): it hangs on a hook inside the pump station, on the lane, and the two flank Bellhands outside the east door are what it is for. Gameplay: the DISPATCH HUT (empty cradles on a rail, ropes still swinging; a gang asleep 10 m outside the door) -> the WEST PAN (gangs either side of the road; optional work parties far north and south hold the loot) -> the FIRST CAUSEWAY over channel 1 (five metres of toxic brine; planks at three places): crossing it (or the two others, or the station fallback) releases a HUNTING PARTY from the hut's cradles behind you -> the MIDDLE PAN (the guard on the station road; two flank gangs, each with a Bellhand, either side of the station) -> the PUMP STATION (roofed; its valve wheel opens the east cradle hall, wakes what hangs there and unlocks the hatch; a SECRET cistern behind its east wall holds an armour vest) -> the SECOND CAUSEWAY over channel 2 -> the EAST PAN (road gang, Gaunts, a Bellhand each side of the hall, far back) -> the CRADLE HALL (roofed, rails of cradles and pods; five Tollbearers and two Gaunts wake when the valve is thrown) -> the HATCH at its east end (the exit is locked until the valve). Beats: the opening objective, the release, the station, the valve. Only existing enemies, weapons and kit; no new engine code. Look: white salt crust under a pale sky, slate road, teal brine, corrugated sheds. 53 enemies (the party adds five); a perfect bot needs about 59 s on normal (measured by verify-map; par 360 s is 6.1x that, a placeholder the owner decides).
(Design lessons from building it: ammunition and health on the road lane ITSELF, not two cells off it, or the first approach runs dry; a Bellhand with a clear line down a causeway kills the bot on hard, so a solid settling tank and a cistern screen the east causeway from the flank Bellhands; two Gaunts and a Tollbearer on the east road were the cliff between a bot that finishes with 40 damage and one that dies on hard; a Sexton in the hall made the bot stall in combat, so there is none; lanterns on the centre lane of a room block the view down it.)

**M03 Rail Yard (main): Moving-train route puzzle under fire.** Bodies shipped inland by rail. Scale class MIXED (61% roofed, p90 sightline 16 m, walls 4.2 m). Introduces SHUNTING: the marshalling shed's east end is closed by a train whose three wagons are moving floors (raised 4 m = a wall; lowered 0.5 m = a flat plank bed); a lever in each aisle (north, centre, south) lowers ONE wagon and wakes the open throat beyond it, and every way east has its own threat (a tower with a Bellhand beside each side exit; the centre comes out on the road between them). Route: the signal cabin -> the shed (two consists of solid wagons with couplings, three aisles with a track each, a gang asleep 11 m from the door) -> a lever -> its wagon drops -> the throat (open, derelict wagons cutting the sightlines, two towers) -> the signal box on the road (its lever rolls up the dock gate and wakes the NEAR half of the dock) -> the dock (the Warden-Graft stays idle until it sees you; pillars on its charge lane) -> the exit. SECRET: a tool locker behind a loose panel in the shed's north wall (an armour vest). 36 enemies, no new kinds; the first non-human enemy is still M05. Look: rusted corrugated wagons, rails and sleepers (new skin), a pale open throat under the overcast sky. NOT a literal moving train: the engine moves floors up and down, not along a track, so the 'train' is wagons that sink; if the owner wants a train that rolls, that is a new engine feature.

**M04 Kiln Row (main): Heat hazards, narrow furnaces, secret exit.** Kilns used to cook Tollbearer shells. Scale class COMPRESSION (100% roofed, p90 16 m, ceiling 3.8 m). Introduces the EMBER BED (floor effect `h`: slows 0.85x and burns 8 dps; drawn as a glowing unlit overlay) and the DAMPER HANDLES (two keys, brass and iron, that the flue lever spends). Route: the gatehouse -> the CHARGING HALL (an ember trench down the middle with two bridges and an island holding the brass handle; gangs on the north and south strips, a Bellhand on each far corner) -> a corridor -> the FIRING GALLERY (a ring round a block of kilns; an ember strip down each corridor leaves a narrow safe lane beside the wall; taking the iron handle in the east corridor opens four kiln mouths (ambush closets, Gaunts)) -> the FLUE CHAMBER (Bellhands, a guard, pods and cradles; the lever needs both handles and unlocks the exit). SECRET EXIT: a panel in the gallery's south wall hides a tunnel to C1E2S01 The Rime Vault. 32 enemies, no new kinds. Look: sooted kiln brick with live orange seams (new skin), clinker floors cracked with embers (new), warm lamps, teal cradle glow.

**S01 The Rime Vault (secret): Cold-storage vault, puzzle-lite.** Pre-invasion Marrow research the Wardens hid. Scale class COMPRESSION, no enemies (`quality.safe`). Reached from Kiln Row's secret exit; returns to The Cradle Annex. Introduces the Wardens' research vault: the first plain statement of what the Works is for (the pans are a cover, the beds under them a receiver; the Vael do not like cold), behind three coolant valves (keys, in alcoves: two north, one south) and one lever that rolls up the vault door; a real cache inside (two armour vests, two large health, ammunition). Look: frosted steel plate walls and deck (new skins), shelves of specimens, cool lamps, cradles with pods in the vault.

**M06 Pump Cathedral (main): Verticality and catwalk combat.** The Works drains something from the sea. Scale class COMPRESSION (100% roofed, p90 sightline 19 m; the nave is 13 m to the roof, declared with `quality.tallReason`: the episode's one named cathedral hall). Also introduces the CHARGE-ARC LAMP (weapon 5; owner's go 2026-10-06): it lies on the east catwalk landing where the cage stops, and cell boxes follow on the lane; chain lightning for the clustered Bellhands and Gills of the galleries. Introduces the VERTICAL ROUTE: the ground is a wading sump round the great iron pump with housings for cover; the ONLY way up is a cage lift (a moving floor, 4 m, with a call lever on a post, `once:false`); the catwalks run round the east end (north, east, south) at 4 m, each with a gallery at 8 m reached by a stair; the drain lever is on the SOUTH gallery and the exit on the NORTH gallery, so the route climbs twice with Bellhands over you on both. Route: entrance hall -> the ground nave (gangs, pillars, housings) -> the cage -> the east catwalk (Gaunts spawn when it arrives) -> south terrace -> south stair -> the drain lever -> back down -> north terrace -> north stair -> the exit (locked until the drain lever). SECRET: an engineers' office behind a panel in the nave's west wall (an armour vest). 44 enemies (6 Bellhands), no new kinds. Look: iron walls and grate floors under one tall roof, a cradle rail hung from the roof, teal wading water.

**M07 Slurry Undercroft (main): Dark close-quarters, light management.** The Hush is thickest underground. Scale class COMPRESSION (100% roofed, p90 12.5 m, ceiling 3.2 m). Introduces LIGHT AS A SCHEDULE: the undercroft starts nearly dark (ambient 0.3); four breaker levers, one per zone, each raises the light a stage (0.5, 0.7, 0.85, 1), rolls up the door to the next zone and WAKES what lives there, so the player chooses when to throw each lever and what to carry through the door; the last breaker also unlocks the exit. Zones: the pump gallery (three sleepers) -> the vat hall (wading slurry, four vats, two dry lanes in a cross) -> the settling tanks (iron tanks, Bellhands behind them) -> the sump heart (pods and cradles: where the line starts). SECRET: a drain room under the tanks' south wall. 31 enemies, no new kinds. Look: resin walls with glowing veins (E1's resin skin), the new slurry floor, pods on the line; E1M07 darkened with one lever, this one in stages.

**M05 The Cradle Annex (main): First cradle-pod reveal, stealth-to-brawl.** The Grafting mechanism shown, not told. Scale class COMPRESSION (100% roofed, p90 13.5 m). Introduces the FIRST NON-HUMAN ENEMY, the DRONE-GILL (owner decision 2026-10-05: first non-human enemy at C1E2M05): a flying Vael hatchling (hover 1.2 m, ignores props and water, keeps its distance, strafes, spits slow spores, fragile), revealed by the Annex itself: the first two bays are QUIET (rows of cradles with pods hanging in them, a few sleepers at the far ends, two inert pods already in the airlock), and at the middle of the aisle the pods HATCH (two Gills spawn from them; two ambush closets in the north wall open on Gaunts): a quiet hall becomes a brawl, and the player learns to aim up. The table room beyond (graft table, pillars, a Bellhand, two Gills asleep over the table) holds the release lever, which unlocks the exit. SECRET: a store behind a panel in the aisle's north wall. 17 enemies at the start (+2 hatched). Note on 'stealth': enemies in this engine wake on sight, so the quiet half is quiet because nothing is placed in view of the lane early; it is not a stealth system. Look: resin walls with glowing veins, grate floors, rows of purple pods, teal glow.

**M08 The Grafting Floor (main): Boss: Graft-Mother.** Episode 2 climax; the production line breaks. Scale class COMPRESSION (100% roofed, p90 18 m: the line's own machinery cuts the hall up). Introduces the GRAFT-MOTHER (owner decision: an Episode 2 boss; design by the build, unplayed): a pod the size of a room at the north end (4.2 m, 680 hp), SHIELDED (6% damage) while any of her THREE CRADLE FEEDERS stands (west wing, east wing and one behind the conveyor; the Cantor's node mechanism, with its own wording on the boss bar), spitting a fan of five spores every 4 s from the start, hatching two Drone-Gills every 18 s from the egg sacs round her (at most four alive), and slamming anything that reaches her. She barely moves and does not back away. Triggers say when the shield is down and when she falls; the exit is locked until she does. Route: airlock -> approach (a small gang) -> the door -> the grafting floor (the bot's `killboss` op: feeders first, then her) -> the exit. SECRET: a night stash (two vests, two large health, ammunition) behind a panel in the approach's south wall. 24 enemies (boss, 3 feeders, 3 Bellhands, Tollbearers, Gaunts). Look: a long conveyor and machine blocks down the middle of a roofed hall, pods and cradles along the walls, a shield drawn as a wireframe sphere.
