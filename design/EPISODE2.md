# Episode 2 — The Salt Works (design brief)

Intent, not status: status is derived from `validation/maps/<ID>.json`.

Theme (from `CAMPAIGN_MANIFEST.json`): industrial evaporation works and rail yards repurposed as processing. Look: salt-white and bone, rust, soot, oxidised-copper teal; open flats under a pale sky, brine channels, corrugated sheds, salt-caked brick. The Episode 2 theme kit (three skins so far, `tools/baker/recipes_e2.js`, seeds in `ART_BIBLE.md`): `L` salt-caked brick, `K` rusted corrugated sheet, `l` the salt crust of the pans.

## Maps

**M01 Brine Gate (main): Checkpoint assault across open ground.** The processing site is guarded and organised. Scale class MIXED (44% roofed, sightlines under 18 m, walls 4.2 m); introduces the FORTIFIED approach (a gate that only a switch in a side building opens) and the first machinery of the Works on screen from the first room. Version 3 (revised after the owner's PT-008 feedback; versions 1-2 were one 44 x 38 m open pan with 6 m walls and a roofless yard). The route: the ARRIVAL SHED (two cradles on a rail: something is wrong in the first room) -> a door onto a short open PAN with a plank bridge over one brine channel -> the north ditch (slow, covered, unseen by the towers) -> the GUARDHOUSE (the kennels open as you enter; the sluice wheel is on its south wall) -> out across the pan, cover to cover, past the salt dyke (a line of crates that cuts the line of sight, so the rank, the towers and the gate are met one engagement at a time) -> the GATE (rolled up by the wheel; pulling it wakes the near half of the hall) -> the receiving HALL (roofed, columns, beams, a cradle rail and pods overhead; the Warden-Graft and its Gaunt wait at the far end with a pillar on its charge lane) -> the GRAFTING BAY (resin walls, pods) -> the exit. Optional: the PUMP HOUSE on the south side (a cache, two sleepers) with a SECRET valve room behind its east wall. Only existing enemies, weapons and kit. 30 enemies (18 Tollbearers, 9 Gaunts, 2 Bellhands, the Warden-Graft); a perfect bot needs about 83 s on normal (measured by verify-map, three seeds); the owner decides the par. Look: the Salt Works.

(Indoor and outdoor are not the same thing in this engine: a floor skin of kind `floor` (planks, tile, grate, carpet, flag, silt `n`) gets a CEILING, a kind `outdoor` skin (cobble, pier planks, slate, silt `m`, salt crust) does not, and a `lamp` prop hangs from the ceiling height. A bridge of the indoor grate floated a ceiling slab in the sky and the road lamps floated at 6 m (PT-007); `tools/maplint.mjs` now fails both. Use `p` for a plank bridge, `j` lampposts and `n` lanterns in the open, and a `put` over an occupied cell replaces what was there without a word.)

(Design lessons from building it, kept for the next maps: an open field of idle enemies that all see each other is a meat grinder for any player who aims worse than the bot, so break sightlines (the dyke); put the ammunition ON the route the first approach takes; keep the entrance to a safe place out of the sight and range of anything that shoots; the robustness gate (a strafing bot with aim error) is the one that finds an unfair map, the perfect bot never does.)

**M02 Evaporation Pans (main): Open salt flats with hazard channels.** Hunting parties working the pans. Scale class SET-PIECE (10% roofed; the episode's one open crossing, never the opener); its reason is a CROSSING UNDER PURSUIT: the open ground has a job. Introduces the HUNTED CROSSING: a hunting party released behind you across open ground, and toxic brine channels that slow and burn (crossed on planks, or waded at a cost). Gameplay: the DISPATCH HUT (empty cradles on a rail, ropes still swinging; a gang asleep 10 m outside the door) -> the WEST PAN (gangs either side of the road; optional work parties far north and south hold the loot) -> the FIRST CAUSEWAY over channel 1 (five metres of toxic brine; planks at three places): crossing it (or the two others, or the station fallback) releases a HUNTING PARTY from the hut's cradles behind you -> the MIDDLE PAN (the guard on the station road; two flank gangs, each with a Bellhand, either side of the station) -> the PUMP STATION (roofed; its valve wheel opens the east cradle hall, wakes what hangs there and unlocks the hatch; a SECRET cistern behind its east wall holds an armour vest) -> the SECOND CAUSEWAY over channel 2 -> the EAST PAN (road gang, Gaunts, a Bellhand each side of the hall, far back) -> the CRADLE HALL (roofed, rails of cradles and pods; five Tollbearers and two Gaunts wake when the valve is thrown) -> the HATCH at its east end (the exit is locked until the valve). Beats: the opening objective, the release, the station, the valve. Only existing enemies, weapons and kit; no new engine code. Look: white salt crust under a pale sky, slate road, teal brine, corrugated sheds. 53 enemies (the party adds five); a perfect bot needs about 59 s on normal (measured by verify-map; par 360 s is 6.1x that, a placeholder the owner decides).
(Design lessons from building it: ammunition and health on the road lane ITSELF, not two cells off it, or the first approach runs dry; a Bellhand with a clear line down a causeway kills the bot on hard, so a solid settling tank and a cistern screen the east causeway from the flank Bellhands; two Gaunts and a Tollbearer on the east road were the cliff between a bot that finishes with 40 damage and one that dies on hard; a Sexton in the hall made the bot stall in combat, so there is none; lanterns on the centre lane of a room block the view down it.)
