## Breathing gates (M02)
A door entry with `breath: { period, open, phase }`: it opens for `open` of every `period` seconds (a different `phase` per gate), whatever anyone does; the Use key does nothing to it (the prompt says it opens when the ship breathes in). It never closes on a body (anyone within 1.3 m of the slab holds it open, as the ordinary doors do), it blocks sight, shots and movement while shut, and its slab is drawn as a resin membrane. Enemies path through it as if it were open (they queue at a shut gate and come through when it breathes); the bots wait at a shut gate until it is open for long enough to cross.
## Spore beds (M03)
The floor effect `s` ("spore bed"): harmless while dormant, a bloom that slows (0.7) and burns (7 per second) while it is on; each bed region has its own `period / on / phase` in the map's `pulses` list; it swells and glows for a second BEFORE it blooms (the warning), and fades after. Vael creatures and the sleeping do not care; only the player is hurt.
