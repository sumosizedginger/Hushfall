# Outside playtest, 2026-11-18 (on ac112d6, the pushed build of 2026-11-01)

Given by the owner on 2026-11-17. Not an instruction: the triage is in `PRODUCTION_LOG.md` (ONBOARDING) and in the answer of that day.

Played from a fresh clone at ac112d6: `npm ci` + `npm run build` clean. Headless Chrome (SwiftShader) through the dev server and `__GAME_TEST__`: manual inputs and the route bot on 13 maps (C1E1M01, C1E1M05, C1E1M08, C1E2M08, C1E3M04, C1E3M08, C1E4M04, C1E4M08, C2M10, C2M12, C2M24, C2M29, C2M30), about 83 s each; a Node run of all 68 main routes on Normal, seed 1. No console or page errors.

**Verdict:** a real, complete, finishable game with a strong premise and a distinctive painterly look, tuned, paced and presented like a verification project rather than a game a stranger falls for in the first ten minutes.

**In numbers (bot, Normal):** 68/68 main routes complete, 116.9 min in all, 103 s a map on average (a human perhaps 4 to 16 hours). No map killed the bot. Closest calls (end HP): C1E4M02 17, C2M21 28, C2M11 29, C2M01 31, C1E3M02 34, C1E4M08 40. Worst damage: Gate Warden 286, C1E1M07 149, C1E4M03 145, Grand Cantor 130, Ferry Terminal 128. Most shots: C2M18 319, C2M15 314, C1E1M08 309. Only C1E4M05 needed the strafing fighter.

**What works:** the premise lands on screen (Marrow Quay at dusk, the teal bell tower); the title screen; palettes change by episode; the weapon models are the best art; boss bars and shield notes are clear; Ines's lines telegraph encounters in character; nothing crashed or soft-locked.

**What does not, in their order:**
1. The first ten minutes teach almost nothing: M01 has no objective although the first-run line points at one; aim, sprint, Use, lamp, melee, switching and ammunition are never taught, only listed in a dense legend in the picture for 6.8 s (17 s on a first run), on every map.
2. Enemies do not read at 10 to 20 m: the tells are visible only up close.
3. Bosses are mostly fought down long empty corridors and the boss is often small or off-screen; most share the kill-the-nodes-then-damage skeleton (Root Bell and Hive Cantor are the exceptions; Gate Warden reads).
4. Map titles wash out over a bright sky or wall.
5. Objective text clipped (C2M10, C2M30 as they read it; C2M29 wraps awkwardly).
6. Spawns face walls (C2M10, C1E1M05).
7. Empty floors in the big halls (Root Bell, Drowned Choir, Choir of One); the Gate Warden's ring floor reads as noise at low resolution.
8. Normal is gentle for a bot and spiky for a human (Gate Warden 286 damage, Wreck Belt to 17 HP); Hard's +25% can two-shot through armour.
9. Roster fatigue: Tollbearer 730 and Gaunt 548 are 60% of 2,138 placed enemies.
10. The ending is quiet: restraint or anticlimax, only a human can say.

**Smaller:** the minimap is mostly black; "NEW IN LOG (TAB)" never seems to clear and competes with the weapon bar; the lamp model overlaps the HUD in the Root Bell fight; seven ammunition types, three loads and six mods are a lot to track, and drops plus the flare safety net undercut Signal House's scarcity; the peaceful vaelfolk are not told apart at first sight.

**What they would do next:** fix the first ten minutes (an M01 objective, teach one thing at a time in the world, the legend to a corner and dismissible); make enemies readable at 15 m; backplate the titles and fix the clipped objectives; re-face every spawn; get three strangers to play Episode 1 blind on Normal and time them before building more.
