For testers
This is a release candidate, and nobody outside its maker has played it end to end: the numbers are first guesses and you are the first real test of them. Play a few maps on a difficulty you are comfortable with; why you stopped is the most useful thing you can tell us.
Settings has an opt-in play log kept on this device only (which maps, how each ended, how long, damage taken, how smoothly it ran, and five optional answers after each map). Nothing is sent: when you are done press Export play log and send the file to whoever gave you this link. A crash shows a Copy button: paste what it copies.
